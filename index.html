Diet Philosophy

🌿 Homemade first — cook from whole ingredients to avoid additives, preservatives, and seed oils.

🥚 Pasture-raised eggs — higher omega-3, vitamin D, and no synthetic hormones.

🐟 Wild-caught fish — better fatty acid profile than farmed. Prioritize small fish (sardines, mackerel) to minimize mercury.

🥩 Grass-fed meat — higher CLA, omega-3, and no antibiotic/hormone residues.

🌾 Organic when possible — especially the Dirty Dozen (strawberries, spinach, peppers, apples).
